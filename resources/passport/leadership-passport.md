# Leadership Passport

*Open Community Leadership (OCL) · POPCOM*

---

## This passport is yours.

Not a test. Not a credential. Nobody collects it.

It's a place to be honest about where you are as a leader, and to watch that change.

### How it works

1. **Reflect.** Six sections, one per POPCOM dimension, ten statements each. Mark how true each one is for you, not your community.
2. **Pick a starting point.** The section with the most "not true" marks is where you start. Those statements show you what to work on.
3. **Check in again.** Every 6 to 9 months, print a fresh set of the six sections and mark them again. Log your counts and compare.

Badges are issued through Badgecraft. This passport doesn't issue anything. It's the paper side, and it stays with you.
